### Research

**Bar strength and quenching in disc galaxies.** At fixed stellar mass, bulge prominence and redshift, are barred galaxies more quenched than unbarred ones?

I cross-matched Galaxy Zoo DESI deep-learning morphology with SDSS MPA-JHU spectroscopy to build a sample of 95,227 face-on discs, then compared quenched fractions between barred and unbarred galaxies inside controlled mass, bulge and redshift cells.

Strongly barred discs show a residual contrast of **+3.69 ± 0.54 percentage points** across 75 controlled cells (DerSimonian-Laird random effects, nominal statistical error only), against a raw uncontrolled gap of +16.88 points. Weak bars show a much smaller contrast. The full-band central sSFR comparison does not reproduce the approximately -0.2 dex suppression reported by Liu & Zhou (2026). The measurements describe an association, not a causal effect of bars.
